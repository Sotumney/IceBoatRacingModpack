## *Ice Boat Racing 1.9.2 Update Notes*

This is a minor update to improve performance and remove some mods that were causing issues.

You don't necessarily need to update, but it will improve your experience!

- Updated Legendary Tooltips

From this point onward, development will shift towards 1.21.4 as that will soon be the default version for boat racing!
